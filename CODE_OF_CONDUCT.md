# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Report issues to **polsinelli@gmx.com**. The maintainer will review and
respond. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban from the
issue tracker and PRs.
