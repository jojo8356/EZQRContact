# Welcome

Three spotlight tips on Home after onboarding. Skip anytime. Replay
from Settings → Read guide.

## Home

1. **VCard QR code** — your business card
2. **Scan a QR code** — camera
3. **Simple QR** — any text or URL
4. **Import a QR** — from a screenshot
5. **Import contact** — from the phone book
6. **Events** — tag scans by fair or meetup

## Collection

Saved cards. Search, import all, export a zip, PDF.

Hold a card **more than 2 seconds** to select several, then delete or export.

## Settings

Dark mode, vCard 4.0, offer your card in return, language.

Full tutorial: github.com/jojo8356/EZQRContact → `docs/help/`
